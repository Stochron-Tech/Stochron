The following are the inputs (for user):
- Scenario selection
- Forecasting methodology (eg: supply chain forcasting, ARIMA, Event tracking)
    Forcasting methodology brings in different information to make the prediction.
- Duration of past data from which they can make the prediction

Output will be:
- Time series of the values in the future and the associated analytics
    This is the prediction
- Link to the explanation of the prediction
- Link to the source of the data
- Link to the source of the forecasting methodology
    [all the links are the existing pages: the user can refer to these for explainability]

Hence, this is what you need to think of when buliding the modules:
1. How it is going to make the prediction
    a. Take in time series from past
    b. Take in scenario info
2. The list of hyperparameters that the user can play around with
3. How the explainability with the build in [how we are going to generate the explanation page]
